3. Why AAAI? Positioning & Fit

Why This Fits AAAI

AAAI-27 explicitly invites:

  • Empirical, integrative, and analysis papers — the scaling law is exactly this
  • "Collaborative bridges" — connecting test-time compute (ML hot topic) to classical iterated regularization (signal processing) is a bridge
  • AI for social impact — the practical early-stopping rule has real edge-deployment impact
  • Responsible AI — the honest self-correction narrative ("we falsified our own hypothesis") embodies scientific integrity

Why This Did NOT Fit NeurIPS

  • NeurIPS wants architecture novelty — the KAN-specific claim was falsified
  • NeurIPS penalizes small-scale experiments — CIFAR-10/CelebA-64 are "toy" there
  • NeurIPS expects diffusion/transformer baselines — our DDPM crashed
  • NeurIPS reviewers are activation-function skeptics — KANs trigger reflexive dismissal
AAAI Advantage: AAAI's broader scope and acceptance of "analysis/empirical regularity" papers makes the universal law a better fit than NeurIPS, where the bar for architecture novelty is higher and the KAN-brand is tainted by the original overclaiming.

9. Narrative Arc & Key Messages

The One-Minute Pitch

"We asked: how many inference steps should an iterative denoiser use? We discovered a universal power law — K*(σ) ≈ Cσα — that holds across 5 architectures and 2 model families. The exponent α is not set by the model; it is set by the data's noise spectrum. This gives a 5-point calibration protocol that recovers 99.83% of oracle PSNR with no per-image tuning."

Self-Correction as a Strength

Do not hide the narrative of failure and correction. Embrace it. The paper's honesty about the KAN-claim collapse is a credibility asset, not a liability. Frame it as:

  1. Hypothesis 1 (v1–v4): KAN's B-spline smoothness creates a unique scaling exponent.
  2. Falsification (Tier-0 audit): Under matched training, all architectures give identical α.
  3. Deeper discovery: The scaling law is universal — it holds even for non-energy models (direct score nets).
  4. New hypothesis: α is determined by the data power spectrum, not the model.
  5. Theory: Spectral regularization derivation gives α = 2γ/β.

This arc is Popperian science. Reviewers will respect it.

How to Answer Hard Questions

QuestionAnswer
"Why should we care about CIFAR-10?" "We are studying a regularity, not competing for SOTA PSNR. The law is reproducible across datasets (CIFAR, CelebA, BSD500). The small scale isolates the phenomenon from confounding factors."
"Isn't this just regularization theory?" "The connection to iterated regularization is the framing, not the contribution. The contributions are: (a) cross-family universality was not previously demonstrated, (b) the phase boundary is new, (c) the practical calibration rule is new, (d) connecting test-time compute to classical theory is the integrative bridge AAAI invites."
"Why is α = 1.376 interesting?" "It is not a magic number. It is the empirical signature of a spectral regularization process. The interesting part is that it is predictable from 5 calibration points and universal across models."
"What if DnCNN beats you at every K?" "DnCNN is a strong single-pass denoiser. We are not competing on absolute PSNR. We are offering a compute-quality knob that DnCNN does not have. At low noise, K=1 suffices. At high noise, K=10 gives +3 dB over K=1."
